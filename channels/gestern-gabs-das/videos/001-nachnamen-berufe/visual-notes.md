# Preview visual direction

23 Pexels clips downloaded; 18 accepted after inspecting actual-frame contact sheets (4 timestamps per clip). See footage-review.json for selected windows and authors. Each MP4 has its original per-file source/license JSON.

Use these as contemporary illustrative craft footage, never as historical records or footage from a claimed German location. Warm restrained grade: slightly reduced saturation, warm highlights, fine grain, subtle vignette; retain natural color, no heavy sepia.

Cut every 3–6 seconds. Vary detail/workshop/wide views, interleave short surname typography. Do not stretch 18 clips into continuous loops: non-overlapping windows and brief purposeful source-backed diagrams are preferable. Muller has fewer relevant sources: two real waterwheel angles plus working mill machinery. Industrial aerials and combine rejected. Tailor cutting and smith details are strongest.

Pexels 6459913: modern sewing machine may show a small wordmark; crop to needle/mechanism, or omit. Pexels 4440945: prefer close net texture, avoid flag. Pexels 38104095: contemporary reenactment/demo, never call historical footage. Pexels 4788511 has an internal cut to tropical reef: rejected.

No generated images, no Wikimedia downloads, no paid APIs used. Search results and source/license metadata retained. Contact-sheet checks cannot establish provenance beyond provider metadata; final playback QA required.
